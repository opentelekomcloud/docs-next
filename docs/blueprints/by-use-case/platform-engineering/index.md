# Platform Engineering

Platform engineering is the practice of designing, building, and operating internal platforms that provide developers with standardized and reusable capabilities for delivering applications. It brings together infrastructure automation, cloud services, CI/CD, observability, security, and operational tooling behind consistent interfaces and workflows. The goal is to reduce the complexity developers need to manage directly, improve consistency across teams, and enable faster and more reliable software delivery while maintaining organizational standards and governance.
